How It Works
  • 1
    Click "Start Attendance" The face recognition window opens on your screen.

    Students face the camera Each recognized face is matched against registered students.
  • 3
    Attendance is recorded automatically A record is saved in the database with a 30-second duplicate guard.

    Stop when done Click "Stop Session" or press q in the camera window.
